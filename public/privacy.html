Gardendoku: privacy policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026.

Gardendoku is a puzzle game. It never asks for an e-mail address, a real name or a password. This page explains what data goes through the game, why, and how to control it.

1. Your game

Your progress (levels, hints, styles, settings) is stored on your device and sent nowhere, except as described below.

2. Leagues and friends

For weekly leagues and friends, the game creates an anonymous account on our server (hosted by Supabase in the European Union): a random identifier, the nickname and avatar you pick, your level, weekly points, friend code, friends list, and the time spent on each garden (to compare it with other players). Other players see your nickname, avatar, level and points.

3. Game statistics

The Android app sends anonymous statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase: levels started, solved or left, duration, hints used, tutorial steps, purchases, videos watched, and technical information (device model, OS version, approximate country). They are only used to improve the game, never for advertising. Turn them off any time: Settings › Anonymous game statistics. Firebase Remote Config also lets us tune the game remotely (ad frequency, rewards).

4. Ads

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob: videos you choose to watch for a reward, and sometimes an ad between levels. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form (by Google) shows on first launch; without your consent, ads are not personalised. Change your mind in Settings › Privacy choices. The "No ads" purchase removes ads between levels. More: how Google uses data.

5. Purchases

Purchases go through Google Play. The game uses RevenueCat to check them: it receives an anonymous identifier and the purchase details (product, price, date), never your payment details.

6. Retention and deletion

The anonymous account's data is kept while you play. To have it erased, write to gardendoku@gmail.com with your friend code, shown in the league. Uninstalling the game erases the data on your device.

7. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or erase your data, and object to statistics. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: cnil.fr).

8. Contact

Publisher: Gardendoku. Contact: gardendoku@gmail.com.
